Rajan Daryani

Post

Mr. Rajan Daryani, a name synonymous with trust and progressive real estate development in Nashik, leads United Builders.

An engineer by education, Mr. Rajan Daryani has a Bachelors in Computer Engineering from Bangalore to his credit. He later pursued his MBA in Finance from Nashik while simultaneously embarking on his first real estate project in the city.

Pramod Daryani

Post

Mr. Pramod Daryani is a key second-generation leader at United Builders, playing an integral role in strengthening the organisation’s execution capabilities while upholding its legacy of trust and quality.

A Civil Engineer by education, Mr. Pramod Daryani brings a strong technical foundation combined with a progressive mindset. He is deeply involved in exploring new construction materials, contemporary design approaches, and evolving development practices, ensuring that every project remains relevant, efficient, and future-ready.

Mr. Karan Daryani represents the third generation at United Builders, bringing a global perspective and future-forward vision to add value to the organisation’s enduring legacy
