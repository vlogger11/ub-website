The Founding

1968: Where It All Began

Late Mr. Asan Daryani founded United Builders in Nashik with a single conviction:
real estate is not a product. It is a legacy.

Mr. Asan Daryani chose a path defined by conviction. He built homes that grew in value.
He delivered on his word. He treated customers as lifelong relationships, not one-time transactions.

That principle has never changed.

Today, United Builders stands as Nashik's most trusted developer — not through loud marketing, but through five decades of quiet, consistent delivery.

Who We Are

Who We Are

There are companies that build buildings; there are families that build cities.
United Builders has been doing the latter since 1968.

Over five decades and more than 100 delivered projects, the firm's work has shaped Nashik's skyline across residential, commercial, and community developments.

Today, the firm's focus has moved, with intention, toward real estate of a different order — developments conceived not for volume, but for permanence. Sites chosen for their irreplaceability.
Designs drawn for the life of the asset.

United Builders is led by second and third generation entrepreneurs. The founding principles travel intact. The discipline of full transparency, the practice of selling only what is delivered, the refusal to compromise on what endures — these are not policies. They are the character of the institution.

The Leadership

The Leadership

01

Mr. Rajan Daryani

Mr. Rajan Daryani, a name synonymous with trust and progressive real estate development in Nashik,
leads United Builders.

An engineer by education, Mr. Rajan Daryani has a Bachelors in Computer Engineering from Bangalore to his credit. He later pursued his MBA in Finance from Nashik while simultaneously embarking on his first real estate project in the city. This rare blend of technical knowledge, management acumen, and hands-on execution shaped the foundation of United Builders' disciplined growth.

For over four decades, he has consistently driven new developments across residential, commercial, and plotted projects, contributing significantly to Nashik's evolving urban landscape.

Renowned for his financial discipline, methodical planning, and meticulous attention to detail, Mr. Rajan Daryani brings an organised and structured approach to every project with his vast experience across decades.
His passion for development is reflected in his insistence on thoughtful design — ensuring that every square foot is purposefully planned and optimally utilised.

Under his leadership, United Builders has earned enduring goodwill by delivering projects that balance vision, functionality, and long-term value, making him a leading name in Nashik's real estate legacy.

02

Mr. Pramod Daryani

Mr. Pramod Daryani is a key second-generation leader at United Builders, playing an integral role in strengthening the organisation's execution capabilities while upholding its legacy of trust and quality.

A Civil Engineer by education, Mr. Pramod Daryani brings a strong technical foundation combined with a progressive mindset. He is deeply involved in exploring new construction materials, contemporary design approaches, and evolving development practices, ensuring that every project remains relevant, efficient, and future-ready.

With a hands-on approach to operations, his strong control over project timelines and delivery frameworks ensures smooth coordination across departments and the timely completion of developments.

Driven by precision and performance, Mr. Pramod Daryani ensures that each project reflects thoughtful planning, operational discipline, and execution excellence, seamlessly complementing the legacy and vision established by the founding leadership.

03

Mr. Karan Daryani

Mr. Karan Daryani represents the third generation at United Builders, bringing a global perspective and future-forward vision to add value to the organisation's enduring legacy.

Academically grounded in design, planning, and management, he holds a degree in Architecture Engineering & Design Management from Loughborough University, UK, followed by a Masters degree in Project & Enterprise Management from University College London (UCL), UK. This international exposure has shaped his holistic approach to real estate — blending design thinking, structured execution, and lifestyle-centric planning.

He is actively involved in introducing new concepts, innovative formats, and contemporary ideas, carefully integrating them across projects to enhance functionality, aesthetics, and long-term relevance.

Focusing on creating signature real estate communities with a firm commitment to legal compliance, regulatory discipline, and meticulous planning, he ensures that every square foot is thoughtfully designed, responsibly developed, and future-ready.

Carrying forward the values of trust and discipline while steering the brand towards innovation and lifestyle-led growth, Mr. Karan Daryani embodies the next generation of United Builders.

04

Mrs. Riya Daryani

Brands are not built by committees. They are built by people with the conviction to see what an organisation could become — and the discipline to close the distance between that vision and the way it presents itself to the world. Mrs. Riya Daryani brought exactly that conviction to United Builders.

Educated in business administration and international business — her academic formation spanning India and New York — she joined the family institution with a perspective that was both global and deeply rooted.

As Marketing Head, she has been the decisive force behind a fundamental shift in how the brand is perceived in the market. The evolution from a conventional developer of long standing to a firm associated with niche, design-forward, lifestyle-led real estate brand.

She plays a formative role in the interior design and styling across United Builders' projects — ensuring that the aesthetic sensibility expressed in the brand's external communication is carried through, without compromise, into every room, finish, and detail that a buyer will eventually inhabit. The result is a coherence between what is promised and what is delivered that few developers in any segment achieve.

Our Vision

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in Indian real estate — not through scale,
but through the enduring worth of what we create.

To build, generation after generation, developments that are worthy of the land they stand on, the families they shelter, and the city they help define.

To hold ourselves, always, to a standard that has no deadline — where the only measure of any decision is not what it delivers today, but what it leaves behind.

Let our developments speak long after we are gone.
